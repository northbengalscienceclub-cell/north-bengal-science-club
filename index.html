About Our Club

Discover Our Vision and Mission

North Bengal Science Club, Rangpur

North Bengal Science Club is an educational and youth-oriented organization based in Rangpur, Bangladesh.

We aim to create an environment where young minds can learn, explore, exchange ideas and develop their potential.

The club promotes scientific thinking, creativity, knowledge sharing, communication skills, leadership and moral values through educational and extracurricular activities.

Through our three dedicated wings — Science Wing, Speakers and Debate Wing, and Creative Wing — we encourage members to develop their abilities and contribute positively to society.
